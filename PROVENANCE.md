# Provenance

The combat experiment and footage use a local snapshot of Gojomons taken on September 10, 2026. The active development project was left unchanged.

- **Engine:** Godot 4.5.1 stable, official build `f62fdbde1`.
- **Combat:** `BattleSimulator` with `apply_effects=true`, which calls the game's shared `CombatResolver` and post-turn manager. The harness checks that effect collection is active.
- **Data:** `data/battle-outcomes.json` contains the individual scenario outcomes. `data/balance-summary.json` is regenerated by `experiments/analyze_balance.py`.
- **Source identification:** `data/source-manifest.json` records hashes of the combat dependencies and content used by the experiment. The full game source and asset library are not distributed here.
- **Footage:** a staged two-versus-two encounter, recorded directly by Godot at 1280×720 and 30 frames per second. The published clip is an 11-second H.264 excerpt with in-game audio. There are no fabricated attacks or overlaid statistical results in the gameplay.
- **Development:** I am developing Gojomons with AI assistance for code and prototype art. The statistical study is an automated experiment on the game, not a study of human players.

The battle footage demonstrates presentation and combat flow. It is separate from the one-versus-one balance dataset.
